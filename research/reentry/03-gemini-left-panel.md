# Gemini command pilot's left switch and circuit breaker panel
Level: intermediate
Applies to: real spacecraft per NASA SEDR 300 Vol II (spacecraft GT-5 to GT-12; the drawing in Fig 3-5 sheet 2 is marked "S/C 8 & UP") [S6]; game: Reentry Gemini manual updated 1 Dec 2025 [S2].

Sources: S6 = NASA-GeminiManualVol2.pdf, S8 = NASA-GeminiManualVol1.pdf (different configuration, GT-3/4/7), S7 = Vol2 Sec2 (guidance supplement), S2 = game manual (see sources.md). All pages are PDF pages, with the NASA manual's own printed page and figure number in brackets where useful. Tags: **NASA document (real spacecraft)** versus **game behavior**. The NASA scans have an OCR text layer with errors; every clause below was checked on the page image unless marked "OCR only".

## Which panel is it and where is the drawing
- NASA name: "LEFT SWITCH AND CIRCUIT BREAKER PANEL", item 3 in "Figure 3-5 Instrument Panels and Displays". Sheet 1 is "S/C 5 & 6 ONLY" (S6 PDF 57, printed 3-21); sheet 2 is "S/C 8 & UP" (S6 PDF 58, printed 3-22). Sheet 2's left panel carries callouts "S/C 9 & UP" and "S/C 8 & 11 ONLY" over parts of it [S6 PDF 58]. It sits at the far left of the cabin, left of the command pilot's main instrument panel, with a coiled cord and a utility receptacle aft of it (receptacles are "immediately aft of the left and right switch/circuit breaker panels" [S6 PDF 60, printed 3-24]).
- The game manual's overview (Fig 2.3.1, [S2 p.20]) is the same drawing as S6 Fig 3-5 sheet 2 (same layout and callout numbers, same "S/C 8 & UP" footer).
- Clean drawings of the control grid (this answers "is there a panel drawing"):
  1. S6 PDF 58 (Fig 3-5 sheet 2, printed 3-22): the whole panel, drawn at an angle, all labels legible at 300-450 dpi render. This is the version that matches the game photo exactly (it has the TV cell and the same cell order).
  2. S6 PDF 67 (Fig 4-1 "Sequential System", printed 4-2): the panel drawn flat, titled "LEFT SWITCH/CIRCUIT BREAKER PANEL". Rows 2-4 are legible; the top row is blurred (cannot be read). It is the best flat view for rows 2-4 and for the ARM/SAFE positions.
  3. S8 PDF 116 (Vol 1) has text but no drawing of the panel read.
- Layout: four rows. NASA text: "Left switch/circuit breaker panel, consisting of three rows of circuit breakers and one row of switches" [S6 PDF 103, printed 4-38]. Row counts in the S/C 8 & UP drawing: row 1 has 11 breakers, row 2 has 12, row 3 has 11, row 4 has 8 switches [S6 PDF 58]. "Eight switches" in row 4 is also stated [S6 PDF 107, printed 4-42; S8 PDF 121].
- Operating convention: breakers are placed to "the closed (up) position" [S6 PDF 71, printed 4-6]. The four squib arming switches are "in the bottom row" and carry ARM (up) and SAFE (down) labels in the drawing [S6 PDF 71; drawing PDF 67/58].
- Which rows do what, per NASA: the top row pertains largely to communications; row 2 to the operation of the Sequence System; row 3 to the Sequential System; row 4 arms/safes squib buses [S6 PDF 104-107, printed 4-39 to 4-42]. (The scan literally reads "The top tow of circuit breakers", read as "row".) Vol 1 says the same [S8 PDF 116].
- Configuration warning: circuit breaker designators differ between S6 (GT-5 to 12) and S8 (GT-3/4/7); e.g. electronic timer CB8-15 in S6 [PDF 104] versus CB8-43 in S8 [PDF 116]. I quote S6 designators and mark S8 usage.

## Interaction with other panels (for context)
- The IND RETRO ATT, BTRY PWR, RCS, SEP OAMS LINES, SEP ELEC, SEP ADAPT and JETT RETRO switch-indicators are on the main instrument panel, "sequence controls ... eight switch-indicators ... located on the left side of the main instrument panel" [S6 PDF 103, printed 4-38].
- The ABORT control handle is on the left side of the cabin; boost/staging indicators (ENGINE I, ENGINE II, ATT RATE, SEC GUIDANCE, ABORT lights) are on the command pilot's panel [S6 PDF 103, 109].

## Verdict: SEDR 200 vs 300
All four NASA documents are "SEDR 300". The game manual's "SEDR 200" [S2 p.6] is a typo (see sources.md for how this was checked). The Vol II title matches what the game manual calls its source ("Rendezvous and Docking Configurations").

## Panel drawing: SVG feasibility
- Good: S6 PDF 58 (Fig 3-5 sheet 2) shows the full S/C 8 & UP panel with every printed label legible at 300-450 dpi render, including row 1 (which is blurred in Fig 4-1); S6 PDF 67 (Fig 4-1) is the flat view for rows 2-4. Together the grid, groupings (horizontal rules/boxes around BOOST CUTOFF, RETRO, SEQ LIGHTS, WHIP ANTENNAS etc.), ARM/SAFE legends and the up/down orientation can be redrawn.
- Caveats: these are 240 ppi bilevel scans (Fig 3-5 sheet 2 is about 154 ppi native over a 17-inch wide landscape sheet), the panel is shown in perspective, row 1 is only legible in sheet 2, and the "CLK LT" label above row 1 and the tiny legends beside the ATT IND switch are only partly legible. The "S/C 9 & UP" and "S/C 8 & 11 ONLY" areas of the S/C 8 & UP drawing differ between spacecraft; which cells belong to which is not stated.
- The game's version matches Fig 3-5 sheet 2 cell for cell (user photo labels vs drawing), so this is the reference for a faithful redraw.

## Source conflicts
- S6 versus S8 on breaker designators and on some functions (e.g. electronic timer CB8-15 versus CB8-43; ATT IND CNTL LDG function; LANDING SEQ and PARA CNTL only described in S8). They cover different spacecraft (GT-5 to 12 versus GT-3, 4, 7). For the Reentry Gemini (Capsule 8+ per [S2 p.6]) prefer S6 for GT-8 and up and treat S8 as supporting only.
- S2 p.6 "SEDR 200" versus all NASA covers "SEDR 300": NASA wins; resolved.
- NASA text says the left panel has "eight switches" in row 4 but describes seven; the eighth (LANDING) is described in S8 and elsewhere in S6.
