# MLB — proposed lesson outline
Level: all
Applies to: 2026 season (S1 = 2026 rules, primary)

Based only on what S1-S44 contain (see sources.md). Rule numbers refer to S1 (2026), with PDF page ranges.

## Basic (written: notes 01 and 02)
1. **The game in one page** (objective, innings, outs, runs, who wins). S1 R1.01-1.06 p.13; R5.08 p.51; R7.01 p.104-106; Definitions INNING/OUT/RUN p.167-169.
2. **The field and the nine positions.** S1 R2.01-2.04 p.14-16, Appendix 1 p.174; positions: glossary S33-S41, Definitions of Terms; R5.02 p.30.
3. **The batter's turn: balls, strikes, strike zone, batting order.** S1 R5.04 p.33, R5.05 p.36-38, Definitions BALL/STRIKE/STRIKE ZONE p.159, 170; Glossary S5.
4. **Becoming a runner: walk, hit by pitch, hits, errors.** S1 R5.05 p.36-38; S19-S26.
5. **Getting out: strikeout, flyout, force out, tag out.** S1 R5.09 p.54-59; Definitions FORCE PLAY/TAG/CATCH p.160, 164, 170.
6. **How a game ends, extra innings.** S1 R7.01 p.104-106; S10, S14.

## Intermediate (not yet researched in depth; sources available)
7. **Running the bases and the base-running rules** (touching bases in order, tag up, overrunning first, leaving the base path). S1 R5.06 p.40-46, R5.09(b) p.57-60; S3 appeals/force examples.
8. **Pitching rules** (windup and set, balk, illegal pitch, pitch timer, three-batter minimum, substitutions). S1 R5.07 p.47-50, R6.02, R5.10; S8, S3.
9. **Fair/foul, foul tip, infield fly, dead ball, catch.** S1 Definitions p.159-172, R5.12; S12, S13, S16-S18.
10. **Modern rule changes** (pitch timer, shift limits, bigger bases, automatic runner, universal DH, 2025-2026 changes). S1 p.5-6 (2026 summary), S2 p.5-6 (2025 summary), S8-S10, S7, S15.
11. **Substitutions and the designated hitter.** S1 R5.10, R5.11 p.74-76; S15, S42.

## Advanced (sources exist, not yet read)
12. **Interference, obstruction, catcher collisions.** S1 R6.01 (p.79+ in 2025), Definitions INTERFERENCE/OBSTRUCTION; S3 Part II interpretations.
13. **Appeal plays and umpire judgment.** S1 R5.09(c); S3 Part II (ex. appeal play rulings).
14. **Ending/suspended/forfeited games, protests, weather.** S1 R7.02-7.04, R4.03-4.04.
15. **Official scoring: hits, errors, RBIs, ERA, stolen bases, sacrifices.** S1 R9.00 (2025 p.117-153); the S-glossary stat pages.
16. **The season, rosters, playoffs, contracts/payroll.** NOT covered by any current source; needs new sources (CBA, mlb.com season/postseason pages, Major League Rules). Gap.
